# Hoursback 30-Day Design-Partner Sprint

## Objective

Make five business owners depend on Hoursback during normal operations before investing in broad acquisition or additional major features.

The central weekly metric is:

> How many pilot businesses used Hoursback on at least four days this week?

## 1. Recruit the right businesses

Prioritize businesses that:

- Have at least two outlets or three staff members.
- Process 20 or more transactions or customer requests daily.
- Already coordinate through WhatsApp.
- Currently chase staff for sales, payment, stock, or closeout information.
- Have an owner directly involved in operations.

Start with one or two categories, such as retail, fashion, salons, repair shops, pharmacies, restaurants, or service businesses. Contact approximately 20 qualified businesses to secure five pilots.

Suggested outreach:

> Hi, I’m testing Hoursback with a small group of business owners. It turns staff WhatsApp sales updates, expenses, receipts, and closeouts into an organized daily record, so you can see what happened without chasing everyone. I’ll personally set it up for your business and support your team for 30 days. Can I show you a 10-minute demo?

## 2. Demonstrate the outcome

Keep the initial demonstration focused:

1. A staff member sends a sale on WhatsApp.
2. Hoursback structures the transaction.
3. The owner sees the sales log.
4. The owner requests today’s summary.
5. Hoursback shows what sold, recorded expenses, payment activity, and items requiring attention.

Do not lead with the broader workflow catalogue, AI advisors, SEO monitoring, or every dashboard.

## 3. Charge for the pilot

Suggested structure:

- Charge ₦5,000 for a fully assisted 30-day pilot.
- Credit the ₦5,000 toward the customer’s first normal Pro payment.
- Continue at ₦9,900 per month after the pilot.

If qualified prospects will not pay for the pilot after seeing the operational workflow, investigate the objection before changing the price.

## 4. Personally onboard each business

For every pilot:

- Add outlets.
- Add authorized staff.
- Load products or services.
- Configure payment methods and closeout rules.
- Connect WhatsApp.
- Send test sales, expenses, corrections, and receipts.
- Compare the first Hoursback summary with records the owner already knows.

The target is a trustworthy daily summary within 24 hours.

## 5. Measure behavior

Track one row per pilot business.

| Metric | Good signal |
| --- | --- |
| Time to first structured entry | Under 24 hours |
| Days used per week | Four or more |
| Transactions captured | Representative of real business volume |
| Parsing corrections | Under 5–10% |
| Closeouts completed | Most operating days |
| Owner checks or queries | Three or more days weekly |
| Time saved | Owner can describe a concrete saving |
| Problems caught | At least one meaningful example |
| Willing to continue paying | Yes |

## 6. Interview customers weekly

Ask:

- Show me how you handled this before Hoursback.
- Which Hoursback information did you use this week?
- What did you still have to verify manually?
- When was Hoursback wrong or confusing?
- What would happen if Hoursback disappeared tomorrow?
- Will you continue at ₦9,900 next month?

Avoid relying on general questions such as “Do you like it?” Compliments are weaker evidence than usage and payment.

Prioritize a requested feature when it blocks daily usage or at least three of the five businesses independently request it.

## 7. Make the 30-day decision

Strong evidence includes:

- Four of five businesses still use Hoursback regularly.
- Three or more agree to continue paying.
- Owners trust the records with limited correction.
- At least two customers introduce another owner.

If one category performs materially better, narrow the product around that category. If customers enjoy reports but stop submitting data, improve capture and staff compliance before adding more reports.

## Operating principle

For this sprint, the objective is not broad growth. It is to make five owners depend on Hoursback during normal operations.
